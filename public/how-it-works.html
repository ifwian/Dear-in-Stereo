How it works

Three steps, and nobody has to sign up to receive one.

  1. Create a letter

    Sign in, choose Write a letter, and paste a Spotify or YouTube link. Add the song title and artist, then write your message. The preview on the right updates as you type.

    Save it as a draft while you think. Nothing is shared until you publish.

  2. Share it

    When you publish, you get a private link to copy. Send it however you like. You can schedule the letter to open at a later time, set an expiry, or close the link whenever you change your mind.

  3. Receive a response

    If you allow responses, the recipient can write back from the same page. Replies appear only in your Responses list, and only you can read them.

Good to know

A link is a key. Anyone who has the link can open the letter. It does not prove who the reader is, and the letter is not end-to-end encrypted. Share it only with people you trust, and revoke it if it travels further than you meant.

Links are shown once. We keep only a scrambled fingerprint of each link. If you lose it, create a new link and the old one stops working.

Times use your clock. You pick dates in your own time zone. We store them in UTC and show them back in your local time.

Songs stay on Spotify and YouTube. We never host music. Players load from the providers, with a plain link as a fallback.
