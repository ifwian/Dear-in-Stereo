From a song to their screen in three steps

  1. Pick the song

    Paste a Spotify or YouTube link. We build the player from the link, and you can fill in the title and artist yourself.

  2. Say what it means

    Write the message, choose why you are sending it, and pick a paper. A live preview shows exactly what they will see.

  3. Send the link

    Copy a private link, or schedule the letter to open later. They do not need an account to read it or to answer.
